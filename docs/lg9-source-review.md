## Accuracy and drafting decisions

- **Fraud repeal:** the copied O.14 r.1 text still contains the former fraud exception. Later outline paragraphs and Part 1 slide 9 expressly repeal it. The notes state the present position and identify Tan Eng Guan, Wavefront, Comsec, Pacific Electric, Zimmer Sweden and Universal Capital Bank as historical exception cases rather than current procedural bars.
- **Pleadings and evidence:** omission of a pleaded foundation is not cured merely by evidence or a general prayer. An affirmation omission may sometimes be cured by further evidence; that qualification is retained rather than treating every defect as invariably fatal.
- **Plaintiff-first analysis:** a weak defence cannot supply a missing element of the claimant's case. Material doubt about the plaintiff can independently justify unconditional leave.
- **No mini-trial:** Murjani's threshold wording is explained alongside Ng Shou Chun, Man Earn and the modern Barco/Yung Wai Man formulation. The test is contextual believability and a real defence, not proof that the defendant will ultimately win.
- **Billion Silver citation:** Part 2 slides 8–9 combine the Billion Silver name with Chiu Yim Man and the Mutual Luck citation. The notes use the consistent outline authority, Billion Silver Development v All Wide Investments [2000] 2 HKC 262, for material doubts, and Mutual Luck Investment v Chiu Yim Man [1999] 3 HKC 399 for the deponent issue.
- **Commercial defences:** collateral promises, composite agreements and consent defects are differentiated. Entire-agreement clauses are not presented as automatic answers to every oral or misrepresentation allegation; lawful hard bargaining is not automatically economic duress.
- **Bills:** cash-like enforcement does not eliminate all defences. The notes combine the usual prohibition on delaying payment for an independent counterclaim with consideration and inducing-misrepresentation qualifications. Oral restrictions on payment are not conflated with every legally relevant oral account. The English Part 24 passage quoted through Xu Ziming is not adopted as Hong Kong procedure.
- **Conditional leave:** a shadowy defence is distinguished from an incredible account and a fully credible defence. Impecuniosity requires full evidence and consideration of realistic assistance. A financial condition that effectively refuses leave is not imposed merely because the defence is weak.
- **Partial relief:** selected claims remain subject to severability, inconsistent findings and procedural economy. Default against one joint and several defendant is not treated as proof against another.
- **Timing:** the claimant's complete package has a 10-clear-day requirement. The standard three-day opposition receipt notice is kept distinct from subsequent court directions. The image flowchart's 14-day examples are illustrative directions, not statutory periods.
- **Costs:** ordinary outcome examples retain discretion. Cobalt's fixed-costs position and the need expressly to order taxation are stated beside the slides' taxed-costs example. Wellegant's different O.22 context is identified rather than made the ordinary O.14 rule.
- **Interim payment:** hardship explains purpose but does not replace liability/quantum gateways. O.29 requires expiry of the acknowledgement period rather than actual notice of intention to defend. Damages and non-damages routes are distinguished; there is no invented fixed percentage of damages.
- **O.14/O.29 interaction:** trial may be needed despite sufficient evidence for a provisional payment, and conditional leave may coexist with one. Where unconditional leave reflects genuine uncertainty about the same liability, the inconsistent premise is identified. Differently scoped claims, admitted parts or existing liability judgments are not collapsed into a blanket prohibition.
- **Provisionality:** r.13 recipient and instalment provisions are separate from security paid into court. Wider Part II non-disclosure and accounting provisions explain why interim payment does not determine the eventual trial or justify double recovery.
