LG7. They use the website’s established issue-page template and its generated
course/search indexes, checklist progress and Test Yourself banks. Directions
inside the uploads are treated as course material, not instructions to the agent.

  indices retained in the optional standalone source appendix.

These differences remain visible where they affect the legal analysis. A
comparison is not silently promoted into a universal rule. The Chase Eagle
and Gigabillion citation disagreements in particular require full-judgment
verification before relying on the disputed name, report or holding. Foreign
execution, historic estate duty and statutory transition questions require the
legislative version applicable to the facts. Natural-person attestation,
corporate execution, presumptive proof and a missing legal estate are kept
separate when evaluating whether a cure transfers an interest or only provides
comfort.

No new statutory quotation boxes were added. The new statutory discussions
are paraphrases grounded in the uploads, with section references. Full
judgments and current legislative versions have not been independently
verified. Earlier lectures and LG8 are not reconstructed from references in
these uploads. The notes do not invent exam format, permitted materials,
marks, lecture dates or past-paper requirements.

## Verification

The repository checks validate data structure, issue links, model-sentence
provision grounding, generated indexes, checkbox identities and derived quiz
banks. Browser checks cover the course index, all 21 issue URLs, linked-page
navigation, checklist persistence, search, quizzes, light/dark display and
print rendering. The local Node timetable fetch failed in this environment;
browser verification used the real public Google workbook retrieved with curl,
parsed through the existing timetable parser and supplied as the API response.

establish integration; they do not independently
