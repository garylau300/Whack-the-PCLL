# LG8 v1.1 (Consolidated discovery source review)

This review records the second full audit of Civil Litigation LG8. The exam-facing material remains in `courseDetails/PCLL8010.js`. The review file keeps source provenance separate so that the notes themselves cite rules and authorities rather than teaching documents.

## Sources checked in full

- `LG8 Discovery Lecture Outline.pdf` (2026–27): all 14 pages, including the administrative page and substantive paragraphs 1–62.
- `LG8-Discovery.pptx`: all 38 slides, including the worked relevance example and the slide-only details on payment records, privilege, client advice and procedural comparisons.
- The Judiciary’s [marked-up Civil Justice Reform text of RHC O.24](https://www.civiljustice.hk/gaz_sub_leg/documents/rhc/RHC_Order_24.pdf) was used as a focused cross-check for rr.7A, 8, 9–11A, 14A, 15A and 17. The site states that this marked-up text has no legal status, so it was used to detect and explain rule-number discrepancies rather than represented as the authoritative current consolidation. The supplied materials remain the controlling scope for the exam notes.

The case-file specimen of Form 26 mentioned in the outline was not supplied. The notes therefore explain the schedules and drafting method without inventing a completed list.

## Accuracy decisions

- O.24 r.15A is expressed as a case-management power to limit discovery. Direct relevance is presented as a possible tailored limit, not the universal test for every discovery exercise.
- The outline attributes the necessity test for inter-party specific discovery to O.24 r.8(2). The rule text places applications under r.7 within r.8(1); r.8(2) addresses disclosure under HCO ss.41–42. The exam notes use r.8(1) for specific discovery.
- Control remains fact sensitive. Lonhro and Sun Yuet Tai support legal control; the recent English Baroness Lawrence decision is identified as persuasive guidance on practical access rather than binding Hong Kong law.
- PDSL1.2 is separated from the basic proposition that electronic information can be a document. The Commercial List threshold does not exclude electronic records in smaller cases.
- Privilege is analysed document by document. Pre-existing source records, copies, third-party confidence, payment records and later waiver each receive a separate explanation.
- The five recognised ways to challenge a denying specific-discovery affidavit are stated together; mere suspicion does not justify repeated interlocutory applications.
- O.24 r.14A is document specific and retains the Court’s special-reasons power even after open-court reading or reference.
- Statutory pre-action discovery, post-action non-party discovery, Norwich Pharmacal relief and subpoenas remain distinct despite appearing on one consolidated page.
- Interrogatory enforcement distinguishes further answers, a just discretionary order for unanswered interrogatories served without leave, and contempt for breach of court-ordered interrogatories.
