3. What is complete and what is source-limited?

Preparation completed: extraction of text from all three uploaded files; reading of the handout and both slide decks; review of embedded-image overview for additional substantive material; organisation into thematic pages; explanation of rules and case applications; integrated analyses; statutory/date checklists; five worked examples; source cross-references; rapid revision table; and an explicit discrepancy register.

The source appendix preserves the complete extracted text, including additional case references that the course itself mentions without explanation. Those short references have been identified as such rather than supplied with invented holdings. The appendix is a source record, not a substitute for the synthesised explanations.

Remaining source-limited verification: full judgments for the conflict rows; legislative wording/versions and amendment commencement; earlier root-of-title lectures; LG8 vendor-rescission clause mechanics; the actual exam format, permitted materials and past papers. These were not supplied, and the pack does not claim to have reviewed them. The completed notes are suitable for course-based preparation, with those verification points visible where they affect analysis.

4. Final self-check before handing in an answer

Can you state the promised estate and affected interest precisely? Have you distinguished registration from validity and extinction? Have you calculated the relevant dates? Have you tested all independent regulatory/proprietary routes? Have you addressed the actual evidence and reply timing? Does your proposed remedy transfer/release the interest or merely promise compensation? Does demolition preserve substantial performance? Have you justified the timing and legal basis of termination? Have you identified the right procedure without treating court action as automatic postponement?
